; ===== Introdução (glifos 1C8) =====

@ 1C9
The Sanjiyan are a race
who mastered the arts of
immortality...

A people who followed a
path of evolution apart
from that of "humans":
the "youkai."

@ 1CA
This story began with an
ordinary boy, Yakumo
Fujii, and Pai, the last
of the Sanjiyan, who
longed to become human.

@ 1CB
By an unexpected turn of
events, Yakumo was given
an immortal body by Pai
and became her "Wu."

Together they set out to
find a way to make Pai
human. It was also a war
against the "youkai" who
craved her immortality...

@ 1CC
And at last they faced
their mightiest foe:
"Benares."

He was the one who had
destroyed the Sanjiyan,
the Demon King,
"Kaiyanwang."

@ 1D0
Before the final battle
with Benares, Pai said
farewell to Yakumo.

Even an immortal body was
no match for his power.
And she did not want to
trouble the many friends
they had made...

@ 1CF
A fierce light engulfed
the streets of Hong Kong,
and Pai did not return.

But Yakumo knows that Pai
is still alive.

@ 1CE
For as her Wu, Yakumo's
life is bound to Pai's.

If Pai were to die,
something would surely
happen to him as well.

@ 1CD
Pai is alive somewhere.
Believing this, Yakumo
waits for the day they
will meet again...

This time, the two of
them will travel the
world to find a way to
become human, and live
on together...
