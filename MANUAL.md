Text only: the original artwork and screenshots are not reproduced. Names and terms follow the in-game English
translation (e.g. **Body**/**Mind** instead of "physical/mental strength").

Each section shows the page of the original manual it comes from, as **〔p. N〕**.

**Greeting 〔p. 1〕** — Thank you for purchasing *3x3 Eyes: Seima Kourinden* for the Super Famicom. Please read this
manual carefully before playing, and keep it in a safe place. (The usual hardware safety precautions on this page
are omitted.)

## Story 〔p. 2〕

**Protect the world — and Pai — from the youma!
Bearing the fate of a "Wu", set out on your journey, Yakumo!**

Long ago, the Sanjiyan clan ruled over the youma. The Sanjiyan are a phantom people said to have mastered the
"art of immortality". The hero, Yakumo Fujii, met Pai, the last survivor of the Sanjiyan clan, and was drawn into
one strange incident after another.

In 1992, a mysterious pillar of light suddenly appeared at Yumenoshima, in Tokyo. At the request of Lee Lin-Lin of
Yogekisha, Yakumo went to investigate Yumenoshima, where he was attacked by a mysterious figure. The one who saved
him was Pai, whom he had not seen for a long time. But the joy of their reunion was short-lived: someone has opened
a hole in the wall of the Sanctuary and is trying to hatch the cocoon of the Taisoei. Hurry, Yakumo! Find the
"Keys of Kunlun" before the Taisoei hatches!

And so Pai, Yakumo and their companions step into a strange world. To drive back the merciless youma, Yakumo sets
out across the world, bearing his fate as the Sanjiyan Pai's "Wu"……!!

## Getting started 〔p. 7〕

Insert the *3x3 Eyes: Seima Kourinden* cartridge correctly into the Super Famicom and turn the power ON. After the
demo, the title screen appears: press **START**.

※ Pressing START during the demo skips it.

---

## Choosing a file 〔p. 8〕

**Starting a new game** — Choose one of files 1 to 3 with the D-pad and confirm with A.

**Continuing a saved game or deleting data** — Choose the file with the D-pad and press A.

- **Start**: play the game saved in that file.
- **Delete**: erase the game saved in that file.

A confirmation screen appears next. Choose **Yes** with the D-pad and confirm with A. The game starts and the demo
plays. Choosing **No** takes you back to the previous screen.

---

## How the game is organized 〔p. 9〕

The game uses three kinds of map: the **world map**, where you choose an area; the **city map**, set in modern-day
towns; and the **dungeon map**, where you run into monsters.

- **World map** — A map of one country, used to travel from town to town. To move, take a vehicle such as the train,
  or call the travelling beast-demon (Takuhi).
- **City map** — A map of one town. It has all kinds of facilities: walk around and meet the conditions needed to
  move the story forward.
- **Dungeon** — Found inside buildings on the city map. When a monster appears, the game switches to battle mode.

---

## Moving around the map 〔p. 10–11〕

On the map screen, use the D-pad to move the hero.

- **Talk** — Face the person you want to talk to, touch them and press A. Their message appears in the window at the
  bottom of the screen.
- **Examine** — To examine a spot, stand facing it and press A. The window at the bottom describes what you find
  (e.g. "There is a treasure chest").
- **Checking the characters** — Press **B** to see each party member's current condition, then choose **Items**,
  **Equip** or **Status**.

**Items** — Shows every item you are carrying (scroll with the D-pad). Move the cursor to the item you want and
press A.

- **Use**: use the selected item.
- **Toss**: throw the selected item away.

Then choose **Yes** or **No** and press A.

**Equip** — Shows the current equipment. Point the ▲ at the character whose equipment you want to see. The equipment
categories appear: choose one with the D-pad and press A. Then choose what to equip from the list at the bottom of
the screen and press A.

---

## The party 〔p. 12〕

**Status** — Shows the current strength and condition of the characters. Point the ▲ at a character and press A to
see their data. Your money is shown at the bottom of the screen.

※ All money is shown in dollars ($).

**The party** — The party has up to 3 members. Apart from the hero, Yakumo, members join and leave as the story
unfolds, and sometimes you may even have to fight them as enemies.

---

## Battle mode 〔p. 13–15〕

While exploring a dungeon, battle mode starts automatically when the party meets enemies.

For each character taking part, choose a battle command. Then point the ▲ at the target enemy and press A.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Attack** | A normal attack. |
| **Item** | Use an item: move the cursor to it and press A. |
| **Run** | Escape from battle. Sometimes you can't. |
| **Magic** | Use the character's special abilities. This costs **Mind**. Choose the art with the D-pad and confirm with A. Then choose how much Mind to spend with the D-pad (each art has a minimum) and press A. |
| **Defend** | Raises defense. The character can't attack that turn. |
| **Regen** | Restores Body. The character can't attack that turn. (Yakumo only.) |
| **Sanjiyan** | Only when Yakumo is in a pinch: calls out Sanjiyan, Pai's other personality. She can only be called for 2 turns; after that, Pai leaves the battle. |

〔p. 15〕 Yakumo does not die when his Body reaches 0: he is only knocked out, and his Body recovers by itself over
time (the recovery speed depends on his level). If any **other** character runs out of Body, the game is over.

Pai normally acts as an ordinary girl, "Pai", but if you choose the **Sanjiyan** command she becomes Sanjiyan for
2 turns and can use powerful magic.

## Ways to travel 〔p. 17〕

1. **Within a country** — Enter the station in town and the world map appears. Point the ▲ at the town you want with
   the D-pad and press A. Then choose **Go** and press A to travel.
2. **To another country** — On the world map, go to the airport. If you have no ticket, buy one, go through
   departure, and choose your destination. Then choose **Yes** to travel.
3. **Riding Takuhi** — Use **Takuhi's Staff** from your items: Takuhi appears and the world map is shown. Point the ▲
   at your destination, press A, then choose **Go** to travel.

※ Some areas can only be reached on Takuhi.

---

## Saving and game over 〔p. 18〕

**How to save** — Go to the hotel in town. Choosing **1 Night** restores Body and Mind. Choosing **Extended** saves
your game data. After choosing, select **Yes** and press A.

**Game over** — If the Body of anyone in the party other than Yakumo reaches 0, the game is over.

## Secret method: making talismans 〔p. 22〕

Talismans can be found in dungeons, but you can also make them yourself using the **Pyramid Book**, the
**Key of Solomon** (book) or the **Book of Almadel**, together with **White Paper**.

※ The books can be bought in shops if you pay, but **White Paper** is only sold by the shop called "999", and only
after you show them a certain item you got from Lin-Lin (the **Seal**).

1. From **Items**, use the White Paper. The talismans you can make are listed: choose the one you want and confirm.
2. The number you can make depends on how many sheets of paper you have. Choose the number with the D-pad and press
   A to make them.
